Organoid Intelligence for Translational Medicine

Our research program aims to develop next-generation bioelectronic-integrated organoids to advance our understanding of human tissue development, elucidate disease mechanisms, and enable translational medicine. By integrating human stem cell-derived organoids with flexible nanoelectronics, spatial transcriptomics, multi-omics, and artificial intelligence (AI), we seek to uncover and engineer the molecular and electrophysiological processes that govern organoid development and functional maturation. Ultimately, our goal is to establish more physiologically relevant human models for disease research, therapeutic discovery, and regenerative medicine.

Research vision: Integrating organoid intelligence, multimodal bioelectronics, spatial transcriptomics, and AI for translational medicine.

Despite significant advances in human pluripotent stem cell (hPSC)-derived organoids, incomplete functional maturation, cellular heterogeneity, and limited recapitulation of native tissue architecture and function remain major challenges. Furthermore, the interplay between molecular programs and electrophysiological activities during organoid development and maturation remains poorly understood. Our research seeks to address the following fundamental questions:

(1) Organoid Intelligence and Functional Maturation: How can flexible bioelectronics and AI-assisted modeling be integrated to monitor, interact with, and stimulate developing organoids, thereby promoting their functional maturation?
(2) Gene-to-Function Relationships: How are single-cell gene expression programs linked to electrophysiological activities during organoid development, maturation, and disease progression?
(3) Human Disease Modeling: To what extent can bioelectronic-integrated organoids recapitulate human diseases, including neurodegenerative disorders, cardiovascular diseases, and diabetes, to reveal underlying disease mechanisms and identify potential therapeutic strategies?
(4) Translational Organoid Medicine: Can we engineer mature, intelligent organoids capable of real-time, closed-loop monitoring and modulation of biological processes to advance disease modeling, therapeutic discovery, and regenerative medicine?

To address these questions, we employ interdisciplinary approaches spanning stem cell biology, organoid engineering, flexible nanoelectronics, electrophysiology, spatial transcriptomics, advanced microscopy, molecular biology, and computational modeling. Our long-term vision is to integrate stem cell-derived organoids, multimodal bioelectronics, single-cell spatial omics, and AI-driven modeling to characterize, monitor, and engineer organoid development and functional maturation under physiological and pathological conditions. Through this integrated approach, we aim to bridge fundamental discoveries in human biology with innovative bioengineered platforms for disease modeling, therapeutic discovery, and translational medicine.
