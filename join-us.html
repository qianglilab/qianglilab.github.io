Join the Li Laboratory

We are recruiting talented, enthusiastic, and highly motivated individuals who are passionate about interdisciplinary biomedical research. We welcome applicants from diverse scientific and engineering backgrounds who are eager to pursue innovative research at the intersection of stem cell biology, organoid engineering, bioelectronics, spatial transcriptomics, and translational biomedical science.

Postdoctoral Researchers

We are seeking highly motivated postdoctoral researchers with a Ph.D. or equivalent doctoral degree in stem cell biology, neuroscience, biomedical engineering, electrical engineering, or a related discipline.

Candidates with research experience in one or more of the following areas are particularly encouraged to apply:

  • Stem cell biology and organoid culture
  • Nanofabrication and bioelectronics
  • Spatial transcriptomics and single-cell omics
  • Animal models and transplantation
  • Neural engineering and regenerative medicine

Interested candidates should submit the following materials to Dr. Li at qxl465@case.edu:

  • Curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Cover letter describing previous research experience, current research interests, and long-term career goals
  • Up to three representative publications, if available
  • Contact information for three professional references

Graduate Students

We welcome highly motivated Ph.D., M.D./Ph.D., and master's students in physiology and biophysics, biomedical engineering, neuroscience, and related disciplines who are interested in interdisciplinary biomedical research.

Students interested in research rotations, thesis or dissertation projects, and other graduate research opportunities are encouraged to contact us.

Interested candidates should submit the following materials to Dr. Li at qxl465@case.edu:

  • Curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Cover letter describing research experience, scientific interests, and long-term career goals
  • Academic transcripts

Undergraduate Students and Research Interns

We welcome enthusiastic undergraduate students and research interns who are interested in gaining hands-on research experience in stem cell biology, organoid engineering, bioelectronics, spatial transcriptomics, and translational biomedical research.

Interested candidates should submit the following materials to Dr. Li at qxl465@case.edu:

  • Curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Brief cover letter describing research interests and relevant experience
  • Anticipated time commitment (hours per week) and expected duration of participation in the laboratory
