Join the Li Laboratory

We welcome highly motivated and talented researchers with a strong interest in organoids, bioelectronics, spatial omics, and translational biomedical research to join our team.

Research Opportunities

Our work brings together stem-cell-derived organoid models, flexible nanoelectronics, electrophysiology, spatial multi-omics, and AI-assisted analysis. We value curiosity, collaboration, rigorous science, and an interest in interdisciplinary research.

Prospective postdoctoral researchers, graduate students, undergraduate students, and research staff are welcome to inquire about potential opportunities.

How to Get in Touch

If you are interested in joining the lab, please send an email introducing yourself, describing your research interests and relevant experience, and attaching your CV or résumé.
