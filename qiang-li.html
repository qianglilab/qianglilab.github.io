Qiang Li leads the Li Laboratory at Case Western Reserve University. His research focuses on organoid intelligence and translational medicine, combining stem-cell-derived organoid models with advanced bioelectronics and computational approaches.

Google Scholar Profile

Research Interests

The Li Laboratory integrates induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC)-derived organoids, flexible nanoelectronics, multimodal electrophysiological recording and stimulation, spatial multi-omics, and AI-assisted analysis to study disease development and progression and to guide new therapeutic strategies.
